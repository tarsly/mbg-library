# Creative-Finance Zero-Down Car Wash Criteria Guide

This document outlines the strict criteria commonly used for acquiring land for express-tunnel car washes via creative finance (subject-to, seller-carry, zero-down), with SBA financing covering the build.

## 1. Demographic Gatekeeper (Phase 1)
Before looking at any properties, the city or town must meet these minimum demographic thresholds:
- **Population**: $\ge$ 30,000
- **Median Household Income**: $\ge$ $50,000
- **Data Sources**: census.gov QuickFacts, data.census.gov (ACS 5-year estimates)
- **Rule**: Exclude any place that fails EITHER criterion.

## 2. The 5 Non-Negotiable Site Criteria (Hard Disqualifiers)

If a site fails any of these, it is immediately disqualified.

### A. Price
- **Target**: $\le$ $1,000,000
- **Borderline**: $1,000,001 to $1,100,000 (Flag for review)
- **Disqualifier**: > $1,100,000

### B. Zoning
- **Target**: Must permit a car wash **by right** (e.g., C-2, C-3, Highway Commercial).
- **Disqualifier**: Any site requiring a rezoning petition, special use permit, or conditional use approval. Entitlement risk is too high.

### C. Frontage
- **Target**: Minimum of **65 linear feet** of road frontage.
- **Reason**: Required for proper ingress, egress, and stacking lanes for the tunnel.

### D. Traffic Count (AADT)
- **Target**: Minimum **12,000** Average Annual Daily Traffic (AADT).
- **Premium**: 20,000 to 25,000+ AADT is highly preferred.
- **Source**: State DOT traffic data portals.

### E. Lot Size
- **Target**: **1.5 to 2.0 acres** (some infill models accept down to 0.75 acres, but 1.5+ is standard).
- **Reason**: Must accommodate the building, vacuum stations, stacking lanes, and exit lanes.

## 3. Additional Strategic Factors
- **Visibility**: High visibility from the road, ideally at or near signalized intersections.
- **Ingress/Egress**: Easy access with curb cuts and a center road median break (direct access to both sides of traffic).
- **Retail Proximity**: Located in or around major box retail or grocery store anchors.
- **Creative Finance Potential**: Seller willing to take no money down until the car wash is built, utilizing SBA loans for 90% of the build cost.
